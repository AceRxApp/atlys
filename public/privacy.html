Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

NxStops collects the following information to provide our travel discovery service:

  • Location data: With your permission, we use your device's GPS to show nearby places and events. Location data is sent to our server to query third-party APIs but is not stored permanently.
  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and display name via Supabase Auth.
  • Reviews and tags: Content you submit (reviews, community tags) is stored in our database and visible to other users.
  • Usage data: We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous usage data (pages visited, features used) to improve the app.

2. Third-Party Services

NxStops uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Places API: To fetch place information, photos, and autocomplete suggestions.
  • Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, PredictHQ: To fetch event listings near your location.
  • Supabase: For authentication and data storage.
  • Google Analytics: For anonymous usage analytics.
  • Sentry: For error tracking and crash reporting.
  • Open-Meteo: For weather data (no API key required, no personal data sent).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To show you relevant places, events, and recommendations near your location
  • To enable community features (reviews, tags, crew trip planning)
  • To improve the app experience through anonymous analytics
  • To diagnose and fix errors via crash reporting

4. Data Storage

Your trip plans and preferences are stored locally on your device using localStorage. Account data and reviews are stored securely in Supabase (hosted on AWS). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Cookies

NxStops uses localStorage and sessionStorage for app preferences. Google Analytics may set cookies for anonymous usage tracking.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your account and associated data by contacting us
  • Clear all local data using the "Clear Data & Reload" option in the app
  • Revoke location permissions through your browser settings

7. Children's Privacy

NxStops is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the app or our website.
